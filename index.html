Research domains

Six threads, one lab

From ballistic armour to circular supply chains — the questions we ask all trace back to how fibres, structures, and decisions interact.

01 — Protective Textile Materials

Ballistic & stab-resistant armour

Lightweight soft armour systems built from high-performance fibres, ceramics, and smart fluids.

 02 — 3D Composites

3D woven fabric composites

Structure–property relations, nano-structured coatings, and CNT-reinforced composite architectures.

 03 — Sustainability & LCA

Circular supply chain design

Life-cycle assessment, multi-echelon supply chains, and carbon-tax-aware circular textile systems.

 04 — Textile Recycling & Circularity

PET-to-fibre recycling

Post-consumer PET bottles engineered into antibacterial, moisture-managing polyester fibre.

 05 — Operations & Supply Chain

Multi-criteria decision-making

AHP, TOPSIS, and Industry 4.0 adoption frameworks for textile manufacturing and logistics.

 06 — AI-ML Based Modelling

ML-driven modelling & decision support

Neural networks, fuzzy logic, and random forests used to predict material behaviour and guide decisions.

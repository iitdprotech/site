A brief history

Two decades of textile engineering research

Prof. Abhijit Majumdar joined the Department of Textile and Fibre Engineering at IIT Delhi as an Assistant Professor in 2008, becoming a full Professor in 2018. What began as work on UV-protective textiles and soft-computing models for fabric engineering grew, from around 2009 onward, into sustained research on shear-thickening-fluid-based body armour — much of it carried out in close collaboration with DRDO since 2012. Since then the group has expanded into 3D woven composites, sustainable and circular textile systems, and multi-criteria decision-making for textile operations.

Milestones
2008

Group founded

Abhijit Majumdar joins IIT Delhi's Department of Textile and Fibre Engineering as Assistant Professor; early work focuses on UV-protective textiles and soft-computing models for fabric engineering.

2009

Body armour research begins

First DST-funded project on woven fabrics with improved impact resistance using shear thickening fluid.

2012

DRDO collaboration begins

Group begins its long-running collaboration with the Defence Research and Development Organisation on shear-thickening-fluid-based body armour.

2017

DRDO collaboration expands

Multi-year DRDO-sponsored project on soft body armour development using shear thickening fluid.

2018

Full Professor

Abhijit Majumdar becomes a full Professor at IIT Delhi.

2016, 2021 & 2024

GYTI recognition

Gandhian Young Technological Innovation Award received for body-armour research.

2022

Appointed Chair Professor for Decision Sciences (Operations Management).

2024

Technology transfer of ABHED BPJ

Technology transfer of the ABHED BPJ (Bullet Proof Jacket) developed by the group.
